FedFairChain

A research prototype for understandable multi-disease prediction using a fairness-aware federated classifier chain. The tool estimates Diabetes, Cardiovascular Disease, and Hypertension in sequence.

General Guidance & Safety
Simple safety guidance for interpreting the result responsibly. This does not replace care from a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Discuss above-threshold or concerning results with a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.
  • A below-threshold model result does not rule out disease.
  • Maintain routine health screening, balanced nutrition, appropriate physical activity, and avoid tobacco use.
  • Do not start, stop, or change medication based on this application.
  • If you are experiencing severe or urgent symptoms, seek appropriate emergency medical care instead of relying on this research prediction tool.
